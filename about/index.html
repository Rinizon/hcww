About

Enterprise experience, local roots, and a relationship-first way of working.

Hill Country Web Works was built to give local businesses the kind of technical help that is hard to find: capable, honest, easy to understand, and dependable over time.

Founder story

From Microsoft to Rackspace to Hill Country small businesses.

Robert Kane spent years solving high-pressure technical issues in environments where clarity, speed, and accuracy mattered. That experience now supports a different kind of mission: helping local business owners get modern, stable technology without feeling overwhelmed by it.

Hill Country Web Works brings that troubleshooting depth into a smaller, more direct relationship model where clients are known by name and supported with care.
