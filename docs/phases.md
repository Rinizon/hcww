# Development Phases

This document breaks the Hill Country Web Works website project into manageable development phases.

Each phase is intended to become a group of implementation tasks in `docs/SPEC.md`.
The goal is to move from strategy and foundations into build, launch, and ongoing growth without mixing too many concerns at once.

## Phase 1: Project Foundation and Technical Setup

**Goal:** Establish the working foundation for the site so design and page work can happen on stable ground.

**Focus Areas**

- Confirm the website stack and project structure
- Set up the development environment and deployment workflow
- Define reusable layout patterns, navigation, and global styles
- Establish baseline performance, accessibility, and SEO expectations
- Set up hosting, domain, DNS, and SSL planning if not already in place

**Expected Outcomes**

- A working site scaffold or application shell
- Shared header, footer, navigation, and base layout
- Global design tokens for colors, typography, spacing, and buttons
- Initial deployment path defined for staging and production

## Phase 2: Brand Implementation and Core Messaging

**Goal:** Translate the brand and business plan into clear website language and visual direction.

**Focus Areas**

- Apply the brand style guide to the site UI
- Finalize homepage messaging, service summaries, and trust-building copy
- Define how founder story, local identity, and enterprise experience are presented
- Gather or prepare logo, imagery, and contact details

**Expected Outcomes**

- Brand-consistent visual system implemented in the site
- Approved voice and tone for core marketing copy
- Reusable content blocks for trust, differentiators, and calls to action

## Phase 3: Core Marketing Pages

**Goal:** Build the high-priority pages that explain the business and allow visitors to understand the offering quickly.

**Focus Areas**

- Homepage
- About page
- Services Overview page
- Contact page

**Expected Outcomes**

- A complete visitor path from first impression to contact
- Clear explanation of the business, services, and value proposition
- Working calls to action across the main entry pages

## Phase 4: Service Detail Pages and Pricing

**Goal:** Build the pages that turn general interest into qualified leads by explaining services in more depth.

**Focus Areas**

- Website Design and Hosting page
- Managed IT Support page
- Cloud and Technical Consulting page
- Pricing page

**Expected Outcomes**

- Each major service has a dedicated sales page
- Pricing ranges are communicated clearly and consistently
- Service-specific calls to action guide users toward consultation

## Phase 5: Trust, Proof, and Local Presence

**Goal:** Strengthen credibility with supporting pages that reinforce trust and local relevance.

**Focus Areas**

- Portfolio page
- Testimonials page
- Service Area page
- Founder imagery, local visuals, and case-study structure

**Expected Outcomes**

- The site demonstrates proof of work and social proof
- Local service coverage is clearly communicated
- Supporting trust content is ready to grow over time

## Phase 6: Conversion, Operations, and Compliance

**Goal:** Complete the operational pieces required for a production-ready business website.

**Focus Areas**

- Contact form behavior and inquiry flow
- Business hours, contact methods, and optional scheduling integration
- Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
- Optional cookie notice and acceptable use policy
- Analytics, monitoring, and basic uptime visibility

**Expected Outcomes**

- Visitors can reliably submit inquiries
- Legal and business-critical pages are present
- The site has the minimum operational tooling needed for launch

## Phase 7: SEO, Performance, Accessibility, and Launch Readiness

**Goal:** Prepare the site for public release with quality checks and technical polish.

**Focus Areas**

- On-page SEO for core pages
- Local SEO signals and service-area relevance
- Image and asset optimization
- Accessibility review and remediation
- Cross-device and cross-browser testing
- Final launch checklist for SSL, metadata, broken links, and performance

**Expected Outcomes**

- Launch-ready website aligned with the success criteria in `docs/AGENTS.md`
- Strong baseline for search visibility, usability, and speed
- Documented issues list resolved or explicitly accepted before launch

## Phase 8: Content Growth and Ongoing Maintenance

**Goal:** Support the long-term expansion plan after the initial launch.

**Focus Areas**

- Blog setup and initial post pipeline
- Ongoing portfolio and testimonial additions
- Content updates as services evolve
- Maintenance, monitoring, and iterative improvements

**Expected Outcomes**

- A clear path for post-launch growth
- Repeatable process for adding content and updating pages
- The website remains current, secure, and useful over time

## Suggested Task Planning Approach

When converting these phases into tasks in `docs/SPEC.md`, each task should:

- Stay small enough for one focused implementation pass
- Map to a visible outcome, such as a page section, a functional feature, or a validation step
- Include acceptance criteria that can be tested directly
- Respect dependencies between foundation, page build, launch, and growth work
