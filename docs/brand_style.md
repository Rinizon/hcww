# Brand Style Guide

## 1. Brand Identity Overview

Hill Country Web Works blends modern technology with Hill Country warmth.

The brand should feel:

- Local and approachable
- Clean and modern
- Trustworthy and dependable
- Technically strong without feeling corporate
- Rooted in Texas Hill Country aesthetics

The visual identity should reflect the balance between professional tech expertise and small-town friendliness.

## 2. Color Palette

The brand colors should reflect the Hill Country landscape and the maroon, white, and gray identity.

### Primary Colors

| Color | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maroon | `#7A1E2C` | Buttons, accents, headings, logo |
| Warm White | `#F8F6F4` | Backgrounds, negative space |
| Charcoal Gray | `#2E2E2E` | Body text, footer, icons |

### Secondary Colors

| Color | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soft Gray | `#D9D9D9` | Borders, cards, subtle backgrounds |
| Hill Country Blue | `#3A5F7D` | Optional accent for links or highlights |
| Sage Green | `#8A9F8C` | Optional accent for nature-themed visuals |

### Color Personality

- Maroon represents strength, Texas identity, and confidence.
- White represents clarity and simplicity.
- Gray represents professionalism.
- Blue and sage add optional touches that evoke Hill Country landscapes.

## 3. Typography

The fonts should be clean, modern, and easy to read.

### Primary Font

**Montserrat**

- Modern, bold, and trustworthy
- Best for headings, hero text, and section titles

### Secondary Font

**Inter** or **Roboto**

- Clean and highly readable
- Best for paragraphs, service descriptions, and UI elements

### Optional Accent Font

**Playfair Display**

- Use sparingly for quotes or emphasis
- Adds a subtle, polished touch

## 4. Logo Direction

The logo should stay simple and clean.

### Logo Concepts

- A stylized `HCWW` monogram in maroon
- A simple outline of Texas Hill Country hills behind the text
- A minimal cloud and code bracket icon
- A maroon circle or badge with "Hill Country Web Works" around the edge

### Logo Style

- Flat design
- No gradients
- Strong contrast
- Must work well in monochrome

### Logo Usage

- Full logo for the website header
- Simplified icon for the favicon and social media
- Horizontal version for business cards and email signatures

## 5. Imagery and Photography Style

Imagery should feel local, warm, and authentic.

### Use Imagery That Includes

- Hill Country landscapes
- Boerne Main Street
- Local businesses
- Clean tech setups such as laptops, servers, and cloud visuals
- Warm lighting and natural textures

### Avoid

- Corporate stock photos
- Overly futuristic tech imagery
- Cold or sterile visuals

### Image Tone

- Natural
- Warm
- Approachable
- Slightly rustic but modern

## 6. Iconography

Use simple, line-based icons with rounded edges.

### Icon Style

- Thin line weight
- Minimalist
- Monochrome in charcoal or maroon
- Consistent stroke width

### Example Icons

- Globe for websites
- Shield for security
- Cloud for cloud services
- Wrench or gear for troubleshooting

## 7. Voice and Tone Guidelines

The brand voice should sound friendly, clear, and confident.

### Voice Characteristics

- Approachable
- Helpful
- Straightforward
- Technically competent
- Calm and reassuring

### Tone Examples

**Do**

- "Let's get your website running smoothly."
- "I'll take care of the technical details so you can focus on your business."

**Don't**

- "Your DNS propagation failed due to misconfigured MX records."

Technical explanations can come later, but marketing copy should stay clear and approachable.

## 8. Brand Messaging Pillars

### 1. Local Expertise

The business is part of the community, not a faceless IT shop.

### 2. Enterprise-Grade Skill

The Microsoft and Rackspace background is a real differentiator.

### 3. Reliability

Clear communication, fast response times, and dependable support matter.

### 4. Simplicity

Technology should feel easy for small businesses to understand and use.

### 5. Long-Term Partnership

The relationship should feel like a trusted tech partner, not a one-time vendor.

## 9. Layout and Spacing Guidelines

Website and marketing materials should feel clean and uncluttered.

### Spacing Rules

- Use generous padding around sections
- Leave plenty of white space
- Avoid dense paragraphs
- Use bullet points for clarity

### Layout Style

- Centered hero section
- Two-column layouts for services
- Full-width sections with alternating background colors
- Sticky header with logo and navigation
