# Site Structure

## 1. Homepage

**Purpose:** Introduce the brand, build trust immediately, and guide visitors to services or a consultation.

### Sections

- Hero: "Modern Websites & Reliable Tech Support for Hill Country Businesses"
- Sub-headline: "Enterprise-grade expertise with small-town service."
- Primary calls to action: "Get Started" and "View Services"
- Quick introduction with photo
- Services snapshot covering websites, managed IT, and consulting
- "Why Choose Us" section
- Testimonials, to add later
- Service area map or list
- Final call to action: "Schedule a Free Consultation"

## 2. About

**Purpose:** Build trust by showcasing background, values, and local roots.

### Sections

- Founder story from Microsoft to Rackspace to Hill Country Web Works
- Mission, vision, and values
- Differentiators such as communication, reliability, and enterprise skill
- Approach that feels simple, friendly, and clear
- Founder photos or local Hill Country imagery

## 3. Services Overview

**Purpose:** Provide a clear, simple overview of everything on offer.

### Sections

- Intro paragraph: "Websites + Tech Support in One Place"
- Three service categories:
  - Website Design and Hosting
  - Managed IT Support
  - Cloud and Technical Consulting
- Call to action: "View Detailed Services" or "Schedule a Consultation"

## 4. Website Design and Hosting

**Purpose:** Sell web services clearly and simply.

### Sections

- What is included in a website build
- Examples of industries served
- Hosting and maintenance details
- Security and performance features
- Pricing ranges
- Process overview from discovery to build to launch to support
- Call to action: "Start Your Website Project"

## 5. Managed IT Support

**Purpose:** Explain moderate managed IT services in language small businesses understand.

### Sections

- What managed IT means in plain English
- Support tiers: Bronze, Silver, and Gold
- Included services:
  - Backups
  - Monitoring
  - Troubleshooting
  - Cloud and email fixes
  - Light device support
- Why small businesses need this
- Pricing ranges
- Call to action: "Get Support for Your Business"

## 6. Cloud and Technical Consulting

**Purpose:** Highlight advanced skills without overwhelming non-technical clients.

### Sections

- Azure and AWS setup and troubleshooting
- Linux server expertise
- DNS and email fixes
- Performance tuning
- Emergency troubleshooting
- Security best practices
- Call to action: "Book a Technical Consultation"

## 7. Pricing

**Purpose:** Provide transparency and build trust.

### Sections

- Website pricing tiers
- Managed IT retainers
- Hourly rates
- Emergency rates
- Statement that every project includes clear communication and documentation

## 8. Portfolio

**Purpose:** Show examples of work as projects are completed.

### Sections

- Website screenshots
- Before and after comparisons
- Short case studies
- Testimonials
- Call to action: "Let's Build Your Website"

## 9. Testimonials

**Purpose:** Provide social proof.

### Sections

- Client quotes
- Star ratings
- Variety across industries
- Call to action: "Work With Us"

## 10. Service Area

**Purpose:** Reinforce the local identity.

### Sections

- Map of the Hill Country region
- Towns served:
  - Boerne
  - Fair Oaks Ranch
  - Comfort
  - Kerrville
  - Fredericksburg
  - Bulverde
  - San Antonio outskirts
- Call to action: "See If We Serve Your Area"

## 11. Contact

**Purpose:** Make it easy to get in touch.

### Sections

- Contact form
- Phone number
- Email
- Business hours
- Optional scheduling link such as Calendly
- Call to action: "Schedule a Free Consultation"
